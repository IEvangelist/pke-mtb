Tip of the Week: Practice builds skills. Extra rides build endurance. 🚵

The skills we teach each week get stronger when you use them outside practice. Pick a comfortable pace and aim to ride for at least one hour without stopping.

Good places to build endurance:
🚲 Lake Country Trail
🚲 Glacial Drumlin State Trail
🚲 Bugline Trail

And don't skip the off-bike work! Planks, squats, and the other core exercises we do at practice help you hold good form when the trail gets long and bumpy.

More time riding means more confidence, better endurance, and stronger skills on the trail. 💪
