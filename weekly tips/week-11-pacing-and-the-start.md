# Tip 11 — Pacing & the Start

- Suggested post date: Monday, September 14, 2026
- Ties to: Race starts and pacing throughout the season
- Note: Plain text below is ready to paste into SportsYou (no bold/italic).

---

Tip of the Week: Start with purpose and race your pace. 🚦

A strong start earns position. The “holeshot”—reaching singletrack first—can help you avoid congestion, but don’t empty the tank!

Race smart:
🚦 Start strong, hold your line, and stay in control
⛰️ Save enough energy to pedal THROUGH the crest—not just TO it
⚙️ Keep pedaling on flats and gentle declines when safe. Shift as speed builds and carry momentum into the next rise
🫁 Once up to speed, breathe, recover when the course allows, and settle into YOUR pace
🤝 Pass safely and let faster riders through when safe

Race hard. Stay in control. Ride your own race.
