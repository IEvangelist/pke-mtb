Tip of the Week: Air is free grip. 🤙

Too much pressure = you bounce off everything like a basketball. Too little = pinch flats and squishy corners.

Start here:
🛞 Check pressure BEFORE every ride (tires leak air just sitting around)
🛞 Lower pressure = more grip on loose/rocky stuff
🛞 Higher pressure = faster on smooth trails

Find your "just right" and remember it. A $10 gauge saves you a lot of walking. 🚶
