Tip of the Week: Drink before you're thirsty! 🚴

If you wait until you feel thirsty, you're already behind. Sip water throughout the day, not just at practice.

Quick rules:
💧 One bottle in the hour BEFORE we ride
💧 A few sips every 15-20 min ON the trail
💧 Refill after we finish

Bonus: Sweating a lot? You lose electrolytes too — and the best way to get them back is to eat them, not drink them. Bananas, watermelon, oranges, and strawberries are loaded with electrolytes and real fuel. 🍌🍉🍊🍓 For drinks, water wins — skip the juice, soda, milk, and sugary sports drinks like Gatorade.

Bring a full bottle every session — your legs (and your brain) will thank you.
