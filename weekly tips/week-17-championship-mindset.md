Tip of the Week: Look back, learn, and carry it forward. 🏆

State Championships are behind us! Take a moment to notice how much you've grown this season.

Carry it forward:
🧠 Think about what helped you settle your nerves
🎯 Pick one thing you did well and one thing to practice
😮‍💨 Keep deep breaths and one-lap-at-a-time focus in your toolkit
🙌 Whatever the result, you showed up all season and earned your spot

Be proud of your effort, thank your teammates and coaches, and take those lessons into your next ride.
