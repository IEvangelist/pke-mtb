Tip of the Week: Dress for the ride, not the parking lot. 🍂

On cool days, it's easy to overdress. If you're cozy before you start, you'll probably be too warm five minutes in, especially once the trail heads uphill.

🧥 Bring a layer for before and after the ride. Take it off when you're ready to roll.
🚴 Start a little cool, not shivering. Pedaling will warm you up; add a layer if you stay cold.
🥽 Earlier sunsets? Try clear glasses instead of tinted lenses. Inexpensive clear safety glasses work, too.
