Tip of the Week: Get up and get loose! 🚴

Sitting down and stiff is how you get bounced around. The "ready position" is your superpower for everything bumpy.

The recipe:
🧍 Stand up, off the saddle
🧍 Bend your knees and elbows (be a springy frog 🐸)
🧍 One-two fingers on the brakes, always
🧍 Level pedals
🧍 Eyes up so you can see where you're going 👀

Low, loose, and ready. Let your arms and legs be the suspension.
