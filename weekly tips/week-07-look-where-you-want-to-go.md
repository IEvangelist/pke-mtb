# Tip 7 — Look Where You Want to Go

- Suggested post date: Monday, August 17, 2026
- Ties to: Regular practices
- Note: Plain text below is ready to paste into SportsYou (no bold/italic).

---

Tip of the Week: Your bike follows your eyes. 👀

Stare at that rock or root you're scared of? You'll probably hit it. Every time. 😬

Instead:
👀 Look THROUGH the corner, not at your front wheel
👀 Scan ahead — spot your line early
👀 Trust your bike to roll over the small stuff

Eyes up, ride smooth. Where you look is where you go.
