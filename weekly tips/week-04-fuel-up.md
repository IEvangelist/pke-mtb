Tip of the Week: You can't ride hard on an empty tank. ⛽

Skipping breakfast then wondering why you feel like a wet noodle at practice? Yeah. Food is fuel.

Game plan:
🍌 Eat a real meal 2-3 hours before we ride
🍞 Bring a small snack for longer sessions (banana, granola bar, PB sandwich)
🚫 Skip the giant soda + candy combo right before — sugar crashes are real

Fuel smart, ride strong, feel great. 💪
